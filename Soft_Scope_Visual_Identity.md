# Soft Scope – Visual Identity Guide

## Brand Name
**Soft Scope**

## Brand Personality
- Modern  
- Tech-focused  
- Confident  
- Clean & Bold  
- Suitable for software & digital solutions  

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Color – Brand Red
- **Hex:** `#E60000`  
- **Usage:** Logo, primary buttons, CTAs, highlights

### Dark Red
- **Hex:** `#B80000`  
- **Usage:** Hover states, emphasis

### Light Red
- **Hex:** `#FF3B3B`  
- **Usage:** Accents, highlights

### Secondary Color – Black
- **Hex:** `#0F0F0F`  
- **Usage:** Headings, main text

### Background Colors
- **White:** `#FFFFFF`  
- **Light Gray:** `#F7F7F7`

### Accent & Borders
- **Border Gray:** `#E5E5E5`  
- **Text Gray:** `#6B6B6B`

---

## Typography

### English
- **Headings:** Poppins (SemiBold / Bold)  
- **Body:** Inter or Roboto  

### Arabic
- **Headings:** Cairo Bold  
- **Body:** Cairo Regular  

---

## Buttons Style
- Border Radius: `8px – 12px`
- **Primary Button**
  - Background: `#E60000`
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`
- **Hover State**
  - Background: `#B80000`

---

## UI Guidelines
- White cards with soft shadows  
- Minimal use of red for emphasis  
- Line icons preferred  
- Colors: Red / Black  

---

## Sections Color Usage
- **Hero Section:** White background with red accents  
- **Features Section:** Light gray background  
- **CTA Section:** Red background with white text  
- **Footer:** Black background with white / gray text
